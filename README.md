# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard | Syntecxhub Internship

An interactive Power BI / Data Analytics dashboard built to analyze monthly sales, revenue growth, profit margins, and customer payment preferences for the Syntecxhub Virtual Training & Internship Program.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project provides deep insights into company sales performance across various categories, sales channels, and payment methods. It helps stakeholders make data-driven decisions to optimize inventory, enhance B2B margins, and maximize net profitability.

---

## 📈 Key Findings & Insights
* **Revenue Growth Trend:** Monthly sales demonstrate consistent stability, peaking toward the end of Q4 due to high-volume transactions.
* **Dominant Sales Channels:** *Wholesaler* and *Direct Sales* segments account for the majority of total revenue (Strong B2B performance).
* **Payment Preferences:** Digital/Online payments closely match cash transactions (51% vs 49%), indicating healthy digital adoption.
* **Profitability:** Total revenue reaches **367K** against a cost footprint of **333K**, leaving a net profit margin of **9%**.
* **Top Categories:** *Personal Care* and *Groceries* generate a significant portion of the category revenue.

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Optimize Inventory Management:** Increase stock levels for top-tier revenue-generating products (like Plastic Mat and Carpet Roll) to prevent stockouts during peak cycles.
2. **Enhance B2B Margins:** Focus marketing and loyalty incentives on the *Wholesaler* channel to secure larger, recurring bulk orders.
3. **Cost Reduction Strategy:** Negotiate better supplier terms for high-cost inventory items to push the profit margin into double digits (12-15%).
